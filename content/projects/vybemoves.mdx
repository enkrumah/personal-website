Vybemoves is an AI-powered movie discovery application for the moment you know the mood better than the title. Traditional search works when the title, actor, or genre is already known. It fails for intent. The product is designed around expressing that intent in ordinary language. It is live at [vybemoves.com](https://vybemoves.com).

People rarely start a movie night with a perfect query. They start with a constraint, a tone, or a sense of what they want to avoid. The product treats that as the request: understand the intent, retrieve films that fit, and let taste sharpen the next result.

<SectionHeader
  id="problem"
  eyebrow="Problem"
  title="Lookup is not discovery"
  className="mt-16 sm:mt-20"
/>

Traditional movie search works when the title, actor, or genre is already known. It struggles with natural-language intent. Someone can know the experience they want without knowing the title.

<PullQuote>Something dark and cerebral, but not depressing.</PullQuote>

That sentence is a preference with a negative constraint. Intent can include mood, themes, era, pacing, emotional tone, genre, combinations of those, and what to leave out. Keyword search and genre filters collapse it into a broad bucket, such as thriller or drama, and miss the part that matters. This is search and recommendation, not a catalog lookup.

<SectionHeader
  id="search-modes"
  eyebrow="Search strategy"
  title="Three ways to trade depth for speed"
  className="mt-16 sm:mt-20"
/>

The same query can take more than one path. Which path runs is a product decision: how much relevance is worth the latency and the cost before the system answers.

<SearchModes modes={searchModes} />

Quick Match, Best Match, and Explore All are the Fast, Smart, and Hybrid paths. Fast is semantic retrieval optimized for speed. Best Match takes those semantic candidates and then asks a language model to rerank them. Hybrid keeps the semantic understanding and adds structured metadata, filtering, and ranking, including the live catalog. The diagram follows that shared system, then the three paths.
