Vybemoves is an AI-powered movie discovery and recommendation application for the moment you know the mood better than the title.

People rarely start a movie night with a perfect query. They start with a constraint, a tone, or a sense of what they want to avoid. Vybemoves treats that as the product: understand the intent, retrieve films that fit, and let taste sharpen the next result.

<SectionHeader
  id="problem"
  eyebrow="Problem"
  title="Lookup is not discovery"
  className="mt-20"
/>

Traditional movie search works when the title, actor, or genre is already known. It struggles with natural-language intent.

<PullQuote>Something dark and cerebral but not depressing.</PullQuote>

That sentence is a preference with a negative constraint. Keyword search and genre filters collapse it into a broad bucket, such as thriller or drama, and miss the part that matters. The discovery problem is mapping how people actually talk about films onto a catalog organized by metadata.

The same query can take more than one path. The modes are a product decision about how much work the system should do before it answers.

<SearchModes modes={searchModes} />

Quick Match is one embedded query. Best Match continues into multi-query retrieval and GPT-4o-mini reranking. Explore All uses the detected intent to search the wider catalog. The résumé names the same three paths Fast, Smart, and Hybrid. The diagram below is the full system those modes draw from.

The interface is React. The service is FastAPI. Semantic retrieval runs in PostgreSQL with pgvector, using OpenAI embeddings. TMDB and OMDB supply catalog metadata. The service is set up to run on Railway.
